# Geometry Sandbox Visual-Truth Materials

These two deliberately small Style-B material additions support the fixed-camera
mockup comparison. They are surfaces only; room shapes, corners and connections
continue to come from real geometry.

## Assets

- `closed-rock-style-b-v1.png`: calm cobalt bedrock plates for the continuous
  unexcavated map mass.
- `grotto-floor-style-b-v1.png`: damp turquoise cave stones and moss for the
  natural fungus chamber.

Both 1024 x 1024 runtime files were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool,
using the two approved Style-B mockups as art-direction references, then resized
locally from the generated 1254 x 1254 sources. They contain no baked room,
border, prop or gameplay object.

As of 2026-08-05 these surfaces are also used by the canonical
`spatial-prototype.html` renderer and its campaign-evaluation fixture. They are
the active rock and fungus-region materials for that path, not a new gameplay
or wall-asset family.

## Prompt summary

The rock prompt requested a seamless orthographic top-down albedo of large calm
interlocking cobalt slabs in the `#20314E` / `#071427` family, with no grid,
objects, glow or focal point. The grotto prompt requested a seamless orthographic
top-down albedo of broad damp teal stones and restrained moss, with no mushrooms,
walls, room boundary or central lighting hotspot.
